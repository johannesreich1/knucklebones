A ring per group kills the clamping problem. With one ring you had to decide what happens to a peak that lives in a higher group — hide it (wrong: that is exactly when you want to see it) or pin it to the right end (better, but ambiguous). Give every group a ring and the question dissolves: the notch sits on the ring it belongs to, and a demotion just changes which ring is bright.

R2 is the one I would ship. R1 is the most striking but the least readable — seven bands means hunting for yours, and the avatar loses most of its room. R3 is the calmest and scales to any number of groups, but it hides the shape of the whole climb, which is the thing that makes a ladder feel like one. R2 keeps the big ring answering "how far am I" and lets the pip row answer "how far is it".

Note the invariant this gives you: the peak notch can never sit behind the fill, because your peak is by definition at least your current score. When you set a new peak the notch is simply pushed along by the fill — one animation, no special case.
