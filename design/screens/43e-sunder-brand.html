What it says: the rune has claimed these. The glyph on the marked dice is the same glyph on the button that was just pressed, at the same hue — a straight line from the cause to the effect that no abstract glow can draw. It is also the most ownable of the six: SUNDER would look like nothing else in the game.
It scales for free. One glyph per doomed die, no geometry between them, so five victims cost exactly as much thought as one — and every future rune that marks a target inherits the whole treatment by swapping its own icon in.
Cost, and it is the sharpest one here: the brand covers the face. The doomed dice are doomed because of their face, and this is the only idea that hides the number the whole rule is about. At 52px the glyph can sit at ~55% and let the pips read around it, but on the smallest board it will not, and colour-blind mode cannot rescue a hidden pip. If this is the direction, the glyph probably has to become a corner seal rather than a full-face stamp — which costs it most of the presence that makes it worth picking.
