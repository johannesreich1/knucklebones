COLUMN SHIELD
A full column cannot be destroyed.
The most on-brand. A dice game should be decided by a die, not by a wheel borrowed from a game show — and every face carrying a mode icon makes the whole roster visible while it tumbles. The catch is arithmetic: a cube has six faces and there are seven modes, so this needs either a die that re-faces mid-tumble or a cap on how many modes can ride it.
