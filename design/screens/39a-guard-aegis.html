What it says: the column is covered, not outlined. The bevel and the travelling sweep make it a surface with a thickness, and that thickness is what survives on top of dice that already carry a border and a bloom — a mark that is only an edge sinks under them. The drop is the point of this one: your die is never stopped, the pane simply answers where it crossed, so the animation teaches the rule (a ward eats a STRIKE, not your placement) instead of contradicting it. Absorb fills the tint for four frames and pushes the enemy die back off the surface. Cost: it is the most pixels of the six, and a pane over a full column dims the dice under it — the tint has to stay under ~16% or the pips lose contrast.
