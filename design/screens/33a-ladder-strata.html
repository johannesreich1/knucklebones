The groups become the list's own skeleton. Nothing is added beside the rows — the boundaries between them become horizons that name the group and its floor, so scrolling the ladder IS reading the ladder: you watch the materials change as you sink from neon to stone. The depth bar up top is the whole season in one slice (segments sized by population) and doubles as a scrubber. A tapped row opens in place — rank, record, streak, peak, and the same group-progress the profile ring shows, flattened to a bar — so comparing two players is two taps, never two screens. The sticky YOU chip keeps your row one tap away at any scroll depth. Costs: the section headers spend ~9 rows of vertical space over a 50-row list, and an in-place expansion must not reflow so hard the list jumps.
