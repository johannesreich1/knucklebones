Everyone gets the ring, flattened. The profile's one continuous fill is the ladder's best idea, so this study gives it to every row: under each name, a thin bar in the player's material showing how deep into their group they stand. The board stops being a static ranking and becomes a field of climbs — you can SEE who is pressing against a promotion and who just fell through a floor, which no other variant shows for anyone but you. Group, name-colour and bar all come from one --gc custom property per row, exactly how the app would build it from core/ladder.ts's groupOf() and groupFill(). The tap deepens the row in place into the full passport: rank, streak, peak, win rate, member-since. Costs: two-line rows nearly halve the players per screen, and the bars demand honest data — a row whose fill contradicts its points would be caught by exactly the kind of computed-pixel test this repo already runs.
