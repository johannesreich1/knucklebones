Why a split. One die owned by two players: the same six, cut on a seam, one hue on the left pip column and the other on the right. The face and its pips are fixed by the design — the profile never moves them. Shipped 2026-09-02 as the app icon (tools/appicon.mjs, the card's own drawing moved there): the default icon is fixed cyan-and-magenta for everyone, and a device that switches on "App icon in my colours" in Settings gets the same die in its own "your colour" / "opponent colour" pair from a pre-bundled set of the 42 ordered pairs. The launcher tiles across this board show the shipped mark. The dark appearance keeps the charcoal ground; only the hue lit under each half of the die changes.
