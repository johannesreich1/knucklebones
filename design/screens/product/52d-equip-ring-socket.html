OWNER OVERRIDE — 2026-08-30: tapping the ring seat opens one action sheet with actions only. It never lists rune tiles. EQUIP RUNE closes that sheet and promotes the Profile collection already on the page into a clearly bounded temporary picker. Only owned tiles can answer; the existing CANCEL button and Escape both leave without changing equipment. RANDOM RUNE MODE is a separate complete action, and UNEQUIP keeps the shared compact-button treatment instead of competing with EQUIP.
The selected geometry remains: at the 384px stage .ringwrap is 206px, the 74px avatar's lower edge lands at y=140 and .gname begins at y=190. The 34px seat spans y=148–182, leaving 8px above and below, and clears the pencil at x=123–149 by occupying x=86–120. The override changes interaction, not the ring, group name, profile spacing or ladder reading.
One implementation: the card wears the production .accseat, sheet, action and picker classes. Its grid comes from accountRunesMarkup(), and WARD/FATE/random marks come through builder tokens backed by the app registries. The sheet deliberately has no library entry and no copied rune roster: it chooses a mode; the existing grid chooses a fixed rune.
The SILVER threshold is historical: reaching SILVER once permanently activates the selected fixed rune or RANDOM RUNE MODE for ordinary ranked matches. RANDOM always draws an owned rune for each match; Rune Ritual makes its own choice instead.
Empty is an invitation, not a second control: the outer 34px seat remains visible and its plus is centred vertically and horizontally inside it. The plus has no nested dotted border. The outer socket alone supplies the boundary and tap target.
