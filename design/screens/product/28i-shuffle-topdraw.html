The hands: the three-card monte's shuffle, played straight. The spread — already in a scrambled order, because production fans shuffledOrder() and a registry-ordered still is the lie — squares into one deck, is dealt one card at a time off the TOP, round-robin, into three piles, and the piles are gathered back in a random-reading order (here: centre onto right, then left onto that) — so which card ends on top is genuinely undecidable until the last gather lands. Then the draw takes the top card of the stack — no refan. A player who tracked the piles CAN call it (the top of the deck is the top of the last pile gathered — here the fourth flick, the spread's third card); everyone else feels the order is simply gone. Beats: FAN 380 → SQUARE 280 → PILE ×6 1380 (230ms cadence, strictly one card in flight) → STACK 730 (two ~290ms hops, gather order random per deal) → DEAL 560 (off the stack top) → FLIP 520 — shuffle portion 2390ms, whole beat 3850ms (today 1250 / 2900), the shortest of the set because the refan is gone. The loop plays the shuffle ~17% slower and holds the presented top card for ~0.8s. Honest re-order: in the app the initial fan order and the gather order are both shuffledOrder()-random and everything between them is mechanical — the top of the deck is decided by the gather the player just watched. redeal()'s icon swaps are not needed at all: the cards physically carry their runes through the piles, this variant's honesty advantage over every refanning cousin (S8 relabels per flick on its REFAN). What dealBeat still needs instead: one order per beat (fanned only — the riffled/settled arrays go), a random gather order, and the inverse map — compose square→deal→gather into a permutation, find which fan slot it sends to the top, and seat the answer in that slot before shuffling the rest, so the watched shuffle genuinely produces the drawn rune. The loop seam: the respread is presentation — the deck fans back out to the same scrambled spread, standing in for the NEXT deal's fan, because a CSS study cannot relabel runes; in the app there is no respread (the FLIP follows the draw). Cost vs S8: the six-rune offer is shown only in the opening spread — no refan means no second read of the roster before the draw — and the draw leaves the deck squared, off-centre at the gather spot, and one card short; the monte read also cuts both ways: a sharp eye knows the answer before the FLIP turns it. Seam: still WAAPI in ui/runedeal.ts — draw() already measures the journey off an element rect, so drawing off the stack is the same measurement off the stack's top card; the one new requirement is that pileDeal()/gatherPiles() (replacing riffle()/cut()) keep their end pose where riffle sprang back to the fan with fill:'none', because there is no refan to return to; restOf() unchanged for the spread; Sfx.tick per flick, Sfx.place per hop.
