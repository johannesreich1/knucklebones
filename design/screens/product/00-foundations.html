Settings picks any two of those seven — they are the DUELHUES roster (state.ts), offered by the two pickers. --p1 and --p2 point at whatever was picked, and every player-facing colour reads the pair, never the raw hue. Fixed accents that must not change clothes with the palette — danger pink, avatar hues — keep reading raw.

The heats are per SIDE: only the side whose player wears gold or orange loses that heat to its fallback, so a doubled die can never pass for their plain one. The fallbacks sit outside the DUELHUES roster on purpose — they can never collide with a pick. Blue, the newest pick, is clear of both heat families, so choosing it costs neither side its true heat.
