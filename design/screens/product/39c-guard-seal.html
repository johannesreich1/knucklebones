What ships offline: the two protections are different kinds of mark, not one mark wearing two hues. A shield is the permanent state of being full: one closed line, no seam, no charge, and no hostile action can remove its dice. A ward is one scoring clasp. While active on an all-distinct column it adds the raw pips once after native mode scoring—this shown [4,1] adds +5; a duplicate pauses the bonus but leaves the mark. A matching hostile placement sends the settled attacker to the centre-facing clasp and burns WARD. PILFER instead tugs its selected die back into place, burns WARD and steals nothing. A full distinct COLUMN SHIELD column may carry both: a matching attack breaks only the mint clasp, creates zero victims and zero BOUNTY, and leaves the gold loop plus every die. A full matched shield, like [5,5,2], cannot be warded. A nonmatching miss or own placement leaves WARD intact. The line stays outside the slots, so neither mark costs pip contrast. Under reduced motion the same distinctions resolve as static before/after states. Ranked still deals no runes; its action protocol is unchanged.
