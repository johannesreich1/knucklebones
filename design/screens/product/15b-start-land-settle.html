The mark the OS already painted does not disappear and get replaced — it moves. It rises from the centre of the launch frame to the top of the page and settles at hero size, and the type and the stack rise to meet it. Nothing appears; something arrives.

SHIPPED 2026-09-03. src/ui/boot-handoff.ts stages it and src/styles/screens/home.css runs it: the webview's first frame reproduces the launch screen — the mark at the storyboard's size, centred, colourless — then rises to its seat in 340ms and takes the player's own colours on the way, with the type and stack 180ms behind. The end state is transform:none, so the mark lands in its real seat on any device rather than at a measured guess; SPLASH_MARK_FRACTION now lives in src/app-icon-registry.ts so the frame the OS paints and the frame the app paints cannot drift apart. Two faults were caught by scrubbing the transition rather than reading it: the start state darkened a translucent mark against a dark ground and made the whole large-scale half of the travel invisible, and the rule meant to keep the mark on screen excluded .splitmark while the hero's actual child is the #homeMark wrapper — so it hid the mark it was written to protect.
