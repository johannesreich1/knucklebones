RULES

Goal

Fill your 3×3 grid with dice. When either grid is full the game ends — highest total wins.

Placing

You roll a die, then tap one of your columns to drop it in. You can't choose the roll, only where it lands.

Column multipliers

Matching dice in the same column multiply. Two 4s in a column = 4×2×2 = 16, not 8. Three 4s = 4×3×3 = 36.

Destruction

Place a die and every matching die in the opponent's facing column is destroyed. Columns line up vertically — your left column faces their left column.

Reading the board

The chips beside each column show its running score, and ×2/×3 marks a multiplied stack. Working out the best placement is the game — but the tutorial plays a guided round with point previews on every column.

Runes

Offline games can deal a rune beside the die in play. Local multiplayer always has all six; versus AI, you can use only runes collected online. None is the default; named picks and random give both players matching runes, while random 2 gives them different ones. In Rune Ritual, both players see the same three runes, each secretly chooses one, and both choices are revealed together. Only Rune Ritual pauses before the duel to choose and reveal runes. Win a ranked Rune Ritual to collect your chosen rune; Rune Ritual ignores equipment. Ordinary ranked uses each player’s equipped rune after that player has reached SILVER once; a player who has never reached SILVER or leaves the seat empty has no rune. Press a rune that acts on your die to cast it immediately; drag or tap a column-targeting rune onto a lit column. A cast is not a move, so your die still lands afterwards. The full roster lives under HOW TO PLAY → RUNES.

Two players

Pick 2 PLAYERS to share one phone, then choose how you sit. Pass phone: a pass card appears between turns and the grids swap so whoever is playing is on the bottom. Face to face: lay the phone flat between you — the top half is turned for Player 2, turns switch on their own, and the bright half with the rotating centre die shows who's up.
