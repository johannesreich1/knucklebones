The bottom of a screen = actions only

Every bottom stack holds actions, never navigation — and at most one is primary: Play vs AI / Play duel (the markup ships “Play”, but flow/menu rewrites it to name the game, so no player ever reads the bare verb), Sign in, Keep it forever, Next duel. Back, Close and Done are not bottom navigation buttons. A stack is as short as its question: the result screen asks “again or not”, so it offers two — NEXT DUEL and one quiet way on in the short cut (Home ranked, Change setup local). It carried three offline until 2026-08-22, and the two secondaries landed a tap apart. Matchmaking's only exit is Cancel, and it is deliberately plain rather than primary — a panel with one honest way out needs no hero button. Cancelling truly leaves the queue.

In match — one control: the way out

The HUD's single button wears a doorway with an arrow leaving it — it was a sliders glyph from when it opened Settings, and kept promising a screen it no longer opened. It opens an ask-card, not a sheet: mid-match, sound and dice faces are not what you reach for. Page Back wears Duel Brackets; the match exit remains a doorway. Offline asks Quit this duel?, with Restart duel between Keep playing and Quit duel; Restart and Quit are matching quiet actions. Ranked asks Forfeit this match? and keeps only its quiet Forfeit action. The pass-the-phone hand-off has no corner control: the whole screen continues, and leaving belongs to the duel HUD.
