WIPE 05 · PUSH

What the platform does. The profile slides in from the right edge as one piece — header included — while the ladder slides a third of the way out and darkens. Nothing is revealed in place; the page you tapped for arrives.

Why the push. The report asked whether the whole page should slide in, and this is that, done the way the player's other apps do it: the profile comes in from the right at 100%, the ladder leaves at a third of that speed under a scrim, and Back is the exact reverse — which is also what the edge-swipe already promises with its finger. It gives up the Neon Wipe entirely: no beam, no duel-coloured edge. The curve is the platform's own ease, 420ms, so the two directions feel native rather than themed.
