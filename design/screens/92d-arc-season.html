Tier up — empty againGOLD III
The ring spans a tier, not a division. Lighting the next of three segments is what a promotion inside a tier looks like, so progress accumulates instead of resetting every twenty-five games — and the ring only starts from the left when the TIER changes, which is the moment worth an animation. Its right end therefore always means the same thing: the next tier.

The gold notch is your season peak, and it is drawn only while the peak still falls inside this tier — that is exactly when it means "you were here, get back". Once you climb past it, the peak belongs in the fact row, not on the ring.

No predicted points anywhere: what a match is worth depends on the opponent, so the only honest place for a number is after the match — the result screen and the history list.
