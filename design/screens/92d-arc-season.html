One fill, always a percentage. The ring was cut into three division segments to give a nearer milestone — but the bar already shows which part of the group you are in, so a division label beside it said the same thing twice and worse. Divisions are gone entirely; nothing functional ever read them (matchmaking pairs on points, the bots on percentile, the ladder on points and rank). What is left moves on EVERY match, which is better feedback than a promotion every twenty-five games, and the ring only starts from the left when the group changes.

The gold notch is your season peak, and it is drawn only while the peak still falls inside this group — that is exactly when it means "you were here, get back". Demoted out of the group, it pins to the far right — your best is beyond this ring — and the fact row names the group it really sits in.

No predicted points anywhere: what a match is worth depends on the opponent, so the only honest place for a number is after the match — the result screen and the history list.
