Why two lights. Every other study makes the players dice. This one makes them light. One die that nobody owns yet — the token's own steel, no owner class — sits at the shipped tilt between my hue on the left and the opponent's on the right, each grazing its own edge of the glass and throwing a shadow of its colour across the floor. It is the roll before it lands, and both players are already in the picture. The face is fixed by the design — the profile avatar never moves it; only the two hues from Settings are the player's. The dark appearance keeps the charcoal ground; the two washes are the lights themselves.
