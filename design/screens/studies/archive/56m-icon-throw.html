Why a throw. Every match begins with a die in the air, so the mark is that beat: my five three times along its arc from the top right — small and faint where it started, larger and clearer as it comes, sharp where it lands — while the opponent's four already sits on the board beneath the arc. The trail is the app's own die at three opacities, nothing drawn, so the comet tail is the same material as the die that leaves it. Faces are fixed by the design — the profile avatar never moves them. The dark appearance keeps the charcoal ground; my hue washes the landing and the opponent's pools under their die.
