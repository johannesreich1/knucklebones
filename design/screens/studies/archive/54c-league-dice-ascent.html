DICE ASCENT: the player does not receive a trophy that could belong to anyone; their actual avatar die crosses the ladder's own group-coloured horizons and comes to rest in the new group's continuous ring. Promotion rises with a light settle. Demotion reverses the route, lands with one restrained compression and rebound, then becomes completely still. Both loops spend most of their time on the finished portrait, and reduced motion begins there immediately.
One deck contract: the result stays dimly present under the full-screen modal. Horizontal swipe, dots, Back/Next and the final Continue are all visible; there is deliberately no close control. BONE's hero is slide one, then Row Switch, Row Multiply and Bounty each receive a separate rule slide using the app's registry icon and real dice. Repeat promotion or demotion always gets the group visual; a permanent unlock explanation is only new once.
Demotion says exactly what changed: SILVER → IVORY rests the selected equipped rune because ordinary ranked below SILVER is rune-free. The selection is preserved for the climb back. The historical-peak pool never moves downward, so Row Switch, Row Multiply, Bounty and Rune Ritual remain unlocked. The fall has weight without pretending earned play was confiscated.
