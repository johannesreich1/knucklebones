Why line art. The other seven studies are the app's glass die; this one is the drawing under it. Two outlines in the two hues from Settings — mine in front on the heavier line, the opponent's behind — with faces the design chose: 5 over 3, fixed by the design, and the profile avatar never moves them. No body, no fill, so on iOS's tinted appearance the icon survives as itself: tinting keeps luminance and discards hue, and a glass gradient turns to grey mud, but strokes and pips on near-black are pure luminance and come back as the same drawing in one colour, the two owners still told apart by front and behind. The dark appearance keeps the charcoal ground and lets the two lines glow; only the faint wash under each die changes.
