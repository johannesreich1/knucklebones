Why snake eyes. Every other study puts more on the icon; this one puts less. Two dice, one pip each, in the two hues from Settings — mine on the left, the opponent's on the right, leaning in. A single pip is the face that keeps reading when the icon is 29px wide, where a five is a smudge and a six is texture. Both faces are fixed by the design — the profile avatar never moves them. Nothing is hand-drawn: two of the app's own dice on the charcoal ground of the dark appearance, with one hue pooled under each.
