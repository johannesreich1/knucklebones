Why a pinwheel. Four dice instead of one, and the layout does the talking: every blade leans the same way and each is turned a quarter further than the last, so the ring reads as a wheel mid-turn, with both owners on it before a match is played. The faces — 1, 2, 3, 4 — and their pips are fixed by the design; the profile avatar never moves them, where the shipped mark changes with every avatar. A plain 2×2 block with a slight slide was tried first: at 60px it read as a grid of tiles, and the leaning wheel read as a spin, so the wheel stays. The dark appearance keeps the charcoal ground; only the hue pooled under each owner's dice changes.
