Why two grounds. The other studies put the duel in the dice and leave the ground charcoal; this one gives each player the ground. The squircle is cut on the diagonal into two deep fields — my hue from Settings lower-left, the opponent's upper-right — and one die stands on each side of a single seam of light. Two fields read at 29px where no pip can, so the mark never loses its meaning at any size. Faces are fixed by the design (5 against 3) — the profile avatar never moves them. The dark appearance keeps the fields deep and desaturated over the charcoal ground; only their hues change.
