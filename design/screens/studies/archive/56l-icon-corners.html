Why corners. A launcher is seen small and among forty others, so this mark spends its space on two big shapes and a void. My six enters from the lower-left corner and the opponent's three from the upper-right, both larger than the frame, and the squircle crops each so its corner is the icon's corner. The dark diagonal between them is the empty board before the first roll. Faces are fixed by the design — the profile avatar never moves them; only the two hues from Settings are the player's. Nothing is hand-drawn: two of the app's own dice on the dark appearance's charcoal ground, each corner washed in its owner's hue.
