WIPE 07 · DEPTH

The calmest option: a crossfade with depth. The profile settles in from slightly larger than the screen while the ladder falls back and fades under it. Nothing sweeps, nothing slides, nothing cuts.

Why depth. If the objection is to motion across the screen at all, this is the answer: two pages trade places in depth, the arriving one settling down from 1.035 and the leaving one dropping to .985, over a plain crossfade. It is close to what reduce-motion already does (a 120ms crossfade), with just enough scale to say which page is coming forward. Cheap to build, impossible to read as a cut — and the least Knucklebones of the eight.
