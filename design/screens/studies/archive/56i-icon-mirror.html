Why a mirror. The board is two halves that mirror each other, so the mark is one die and its reflection: mine standing on a black glass floor, the opponent's the same five upside down beneath the horizon, in their hue, fading as it goes deeper. Both faces are fixed by the design — the profile avatar never moves them; only the two hues from Settings are the player's. Nothing on the mark is hand-drawn but the horizon line: two of the app's own dice, one flipped and masked by its wrapper. The dark appearance keeps the charcoal ground above the line and a deeper black below it; only the hue washes change.
