Why a knockout. The shipped mark is one die that follows the profile avatar, so 42 players own 42 launchers. This one is the capture itself: mine landing in front, the opponent's behind it caught at the dying keyframe's peak brightness with its pips flying loose. Both faces are the design's — a three over a three, because a match is what knocks a die out, and a three keeps every pip of both dice visible where the glass of one die crosses the other — and the profile never moves them: a player picks an avatar, not a launcher. The dark appearance keeps the charcoal ground; the magenta flash under the bursting die is the only thing that brightens.
