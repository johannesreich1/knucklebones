Why a column. The board is three columns of three slots and the game is scored one column at a time, so this mark is one of mine at the moment that decides it: a matched pair of fives wearing the gold the board gives a pair, a plain two beneath them, and the opponent's five turned toward the column — the one die that would wipe the pair out. Every face is fixed by the design: 5·5·2 against a 5, chosen for the story they tell together, and the profile avatar never moves them. Nothing on the mark is drawn by hand — four of the app's own dice on the charcoal ground of the dark appearance; the gold, cyan and magenta washes under each owner are the only hue it carries.
