Why a cube. The shipped mark is one flat face that follows the profile avatar, so 42 players own 42 launchers. This one is the die itself: three real faces folded into a solid at the 1-2-3 corner, the way a die sits on the table. The faces and their pips are fixed by the design — the profile never moves them. Mine is the lit top and the left; the opponent's hue is the whole right face, which still reads at 60px, where a magenta rim light raking a cyan face only washed its three pips white. The body between the faces is a hair wider than they are, so each edge is a chamfer rather than two owners' outlines stacked. The dark appearance keeps the charcoal ground; only the hue wash under each owner's side changes.
