What it says: this is permanent. The mode's one unusual promise is that the bank survives everything — including your own board being destroyed later — and a rising number cannot promise that, because every other number on this screen goes both ways. A cut mark can only accumulate, so the row itself is the guarantee.
It is the cheapest idea in the set. No travel, no second rect, no per-die object, nothing added to the board — a strike that kills six dice cuts six notches in the same 200ms, in a plate lane that is already reserved for the whole match (ui/game/scores.ts holds it open so the score cluster cannot move).
It keeps the float, because on its own the ledger does not say WHICH strike paid: "+2 ✦" over the column that struck is the receipt, and the notches are the balance.
Cost: a notch row does not scale. Late BOUNTY games bank double figures, and 14 notches in a 24px-high nameplate is a smear — it needs a fold (five notches then a bundled mark, or notches up to eight and a number after), and that fold is a second thing to design. It also loses the exact count at a glance, which a number gives for free.
