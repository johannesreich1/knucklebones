What it says: twenty-four, and here is where they all are. A single falling counter is a half-picture; the pair is the whole match at a glance, and it makes the mode's arithmetic (nine on the boards, one in play, fourteen to come) something you can see rather than something you would have to work out.
It gives FATE's discard a home. Throwing a die back under LIMITED costs the bag a die permanently — the one interaction between a spell and a mode that has no picture today, and one users have asked about. The discard flying right, not left, settles it silently.
It reuses the bag exactly. Same shells, same fan, mirrored: no second component, no new vocabulary.
Cost: it takes the slot the castable rune lives in. styles/game/spells.css puts .spells at left:100% of the stage row, opposite the bag on purpose, so a spent pile there collides with every offline game that brought a rune — and in landscape the rune is already forced above the stage by the 116px lane. It is also the least useful information in the set: the player can compute "spent" from what is on the boards, and the count that decides the ending is the one that was already there.
