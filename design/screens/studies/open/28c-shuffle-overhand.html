The hands: a real overhand shuffle. The fan gathers into one squared deck held right of centre; the off-hand thumb drags a two-card packet out the back (it ducks behind the stack, exactly where a bottom packet is), hops it over the top, and lays it on the front of a growing pile in the other hand. Three peels move the whole deck — the held stack visibly sinks a card thickness as each packet leaves, the pile dips as each packet lands on it — then the pile squares and the same hand fans it back onto the felt, card 1 first, the top cards sweeping across the whole fan. Peeling from the back means every landing packet genuinely covers the pile under it in paint order — no z tricks. Beats (ms): FAN 380 · GATHER 340 · PEEL 500 ×3 · SQUARE 140 · REFAN 520 → shuffle 2500; DEAL 560 · FLIP 520 unchanged → 3960 fan→flip.
