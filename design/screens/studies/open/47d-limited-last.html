What it says: it is nearly over. The mode's rule is an END CONDITION — the bag stops the match, full boards or not — and an end condition that arrives without warning is the one thing a player will call unfair. A curve gives the last four turns the weight they carry in the play, and gives the first twenty the silence they deserve.
It costs nothing for most of the match. No animation at all above six left, which is the opposite of every other idea here and the reason it can coexist with any of them: this is a STATE, not a beat, so LI1's draw or LI2's tower can run underneath it.
It reuses meanings the game already has. Gold for "pay attention", the mode's own hue for the mode, and the closed ring the protected column wears for "this is final".
Cost: it says nothing on nineteen of the twenty-four draws, so on its own it leaves the mode's identity as invisible as it is today — this is a partner idea, not a whole answer. Escalating colour also needs care against colour blind mode, where the duel pair is pinned to cyan-and-gold: a gold number at six left and a violet one at three has to stay legible when the player's own dice are gold.
