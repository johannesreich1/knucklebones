What it says: an equipped rune is part of your standing. Not a rival to EQ4 — The ring socket but its other half: EQ4 cuts the seat into the ladder ring's mouth on the Profile, where the rune is chosen, and this carries the identical seat into the identity plate on Home — the block that already holds your position and the button that moves it (identity.css, design 13d). The mark is round for that reason: it is EQ4's disc at EQ4's 34px, with EQ4's three states drawn from EQ4's values. A square chip on Home and a disc on the Profile would be two marks meaning one thing, which CLAUDE.md names as a design failure.
Why it survives: the gate needs no sentence on Home. The plate already carries .gpill and the seat lands 19px past it — where EQ4's waiting seat must HANG a SILVER pill under itself to name the threshold, as the state row above does. The seat is a door here as it is there, onto the same sheet in its three presentations, so Home adds no runtime seam of its own; and the frame is two buttons because #homeChip is already a <button> and a button cannot nest a button, so the border and ground move out to a wrapper and each control keeps its own hit area and its own 44px lane.
Cost: measured at the 384px stage, the name's lane falls from 118px to 86px — not the disc's doing, since a round 34px seat takes the lane the 34px chip took. FrostLynx303 is 12 characters and 89px of ink: the shipped lane draws it whole, this one fits 11, so the name is clipped in all three plates above. The claim box allows 16 (account-screen.ts:270), and localization.md:176-182 names plates as a translation pressure point. The row also gives up its chevron and stops announcing itself as a door, and fillPlate paints three contexts from one spec, so PlateSpec grows a rune field the result screen's two plates must be told to draw inert.
The state easiest to get wrong: waiting, and it may not differ from IN PLAY by shade — a merely dimmed mark reads as one in shadow, never as one that is waiting. So all three differences are changes of kind, EQ4's three: chroma (#464c5c frame, --dim glyph), footprint (the bloom removed outright, the silhouette ending at its own edge), and the pill. The edge itself stays full, because the rune is out of play but the control is still a door. That row borrows a foil, EmberCrow896 at 465 points in BONE, since our reader is SILVER. Three states are deliberately undrawn: signed out has no plate at all, not-held cannot reach a seat that holds only what the account owns, and the first rune sealing itself IS the equipped frame — the loop shows the repaint; announcing the moment is EQ8's job.
