What it says: the loadout is not a setting, it is a mark on you. The rune is dropped on your own die — the gesture the game already teaches in shipped copy, since FATE, NUDGE and SUNDER all aim with Drop it on your die (en/game.ts:232,239,254). Nothing else grows a control, because paintAvatar already carries one die to the profile ring at 74px, the reveal’s .dav at 44px and every plate at 18px (ui/plate.ts:80): queue, reveal, home chip and both result rows are marked the day the mark exists. The collection stays exactly what shell.ts:96-98 says it is.
The SILVER gate is a light, not a veil. Below SILVER the etch is in the bone at full depth and full legibility and simply does not shine — no banner, no veil, no words. A gate made of shade alone is what the dropped wax-seal candidate lost its slot for: a desaturated mark reads as a mark in shadow, never as an absent one (29e-rail-seal.html:141-143).
Cost, and there are five. The avatar is chosen identity and this writes ladder state onto it. 18px is a smudge — the size row is the measurement, not a boast — leaving a hue-only rim in a game whose colour-blind mode pins the duel pair. It lies on the pips, not under them: the face is a translucent wash over near-black, so a groove behind it has nothing left to darken, and at 46% the mark covers the centre pip of face 5 — a die whose pips are its number can then be read as a different face. Boot order: paintAvatar runs before any online code exists, so the mark drags the rune registry into the eager bundle. And makeDie paints every rolled die too: one pixel of leak is a rune that looks cast.
The state easiest to get wrong: nothing equipped and not collected are the same plain die — the first and fourth stills are identical on purpose — and because the equipped slot loses its glow rather than gaining an announcement, the collection cannot answer “which one am I carrying?” either. Nothing-equipped is still the cheapest none available: it is what every player has today.
Rune Ritual: the die keeps its mark while the HUD chip reads the loaned rune (docs/SPELLS.md, Ranked boundary). That disagreement is what “never overwritten” looks like on a screen.
