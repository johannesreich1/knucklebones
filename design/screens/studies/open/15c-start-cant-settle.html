A3 · CANT AND SETTLE

The mark already leans — the launcher generator bakes a clockwise cant into the die so the tile reads as a throw, not a badge. Here it arrives nine degrees past that rest and unwinds into it, overshooting once. The die was thrown; this is it landing.

It animates something the mark already claims. The cant is shipped, in tools/appicon.mjs, and until now it has been a static pose. Turning it into the END of a movement costs no new design vocabulary: the resting angle does not change, so the tile the player tapped and the hero they land on are the same picture — this card only says how it got there. Why nine degrees and not thirty. A logo that turns far enough to read as rotation is a spinner, and a spinner at boot promises loading that is not happening. Card 35 already made this mistake once — its second beat used to wobble the launch die "waking into the loader" until someone noticed nothing draws a loader at boot — and the fix was to delete the motion, not to slow it. Nine degrees is under the threshold where the eye calls it a spin and over the one where it calls it nothing. The overshoot is the whole quality bar. −1.2° past rest, then back, is what separates a die with weight from an image being transformed. Take it out and the card still works and stops being good. What it does not say. Nothing about the seam, nothing about two owners, nothing about the split — it is a motion study of a shape, and a player who has never noticed the die is divided will not notice it here either. A1 says the opposite thing at the same moment. Reduced motion has no fallback. A rotation degrades to no rotation; there is no quieter version, so on that setting this card IS the hard cut it was written to replace.
