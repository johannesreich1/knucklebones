What it says: two dice, two points. BOUNTY banks one per DIE, never per pip, and that is the single thing players get wrong about it — a 6 and a 1 are worth the same ✦. A coin per corpse, counted onto the tally one at a time, is the only idea here that states the rule rather than the result.
It gives the mode a direction. The points leave THEIR half and land on YOUR plate, so the bank is visibly fed by destruction rather than appearing beside your score out of nowhere.
It replaces the floating number. Today the game prints "+2 ✦" over the striking column and updates the tally silently; a coin that arrives makes the float redundant, which is one fewer thing in the lane.
Cost: the longest flight in the game — a full board-height, resolved from two rects at strike time, and one object per destroyed die. A SUNDER-widened strike under BOUNTY can kill six dice at once, so this must degrade gracefully: cap the coins, or throw one coin carrying "×6".
