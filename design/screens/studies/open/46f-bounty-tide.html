What it says: what they lost, you keep. It is the only idea in the set that is about how the mode FEELS rather than what it scores, and BOUNTY is the mode most in need of that: mechanically it is a small permanent trickle, and the trickle is what makes it dull to look at.
It is already in the game's vocabulary. ui/fx burst() throws particles on every multiplied placement and every strike; this is the same engine given a destination, so it costs no new concept and inherits the reduced-motion skip for free.
It never has to scale. Six dead dice is more embers, not a redesign — the one thing a counting idea cannot promise.
Cost: it does not count, so it cannot carry the mode alone: it needs the float or a ticking tally beside it, and the moment it has one, the question is whether the embers are earning their frame budget. Four particles per die crossing the whole board is also the heaviest thing here on a low-end phone, and the drift passes straight through the centre stage — the die in play, the status line and, in a spell game, the rune rail.
