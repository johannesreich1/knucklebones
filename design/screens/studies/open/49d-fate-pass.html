What it says: one for another, right now. Simultaneity is the reading: nothing is lost and then replaced, it is exchanged, which is what a two-charge rune you may cast on an ordinary turn should feel like — brisk, not ceremonial.
It never leaves the stage empty, so the player's eye has something to hold onto for the whole cast; the roll animation that normally owns this box always has a die in it too.
It is the easiest to shorten. Both halves are a single translate, so the whole cast can be 180ms if playtesting says the turn cannot afford 300 — the one idea in the set with no sequence to protect.
Cost: it needs 70px of clear lane on BOTH sides, which is the worst case for this stage: the LIMITED bag sits in the left one and the castable rune in the right one, so under LIMITED-with-a-rune both ends of the pass fly straight through furniture. It also says nothing about where either die came from or went, so the supply stays abstract — the thing FA1 spends its whole lane on.
