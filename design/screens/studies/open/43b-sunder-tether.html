What it says: the cast has taken aim, and the aim is drawn. Of the six this is the only one that shows the relation between the charged die and its victims instead of marking them independently — which is what SUNDER is: one die, reaching.
The refused thread is the point. It leaves for the shielded column like the others and frays out against the ring. The other five say "spared" by showing nothing, which is correct but silent; this one separates denied from never asked, and that distinction is the most confusing thing about casting SUNDER under COLUMN SHIELD.
Cost — and it is the real one. Curves between two moving rects are the most expensive thing in this set. They have to be re-authored on every layout change the board can make (rotation, the landscape swap, face-to-face turning a half upside down), and a thread crossing the centre will run under the status line. It also crowds fast: five victims across three columns is five curves through a 168px board. Prototype it against the worst board, not this one.
