What it says: fourteen. The tower is the count, not a picture of a bag that happens to be near the count — and unlike the four-layer fan it is different on every one of the twenty-four draws, which is the difference between a component that is alive and one that is decorative.
The endgame arrives visibly. The last six dice are marked at the base in the mode's own hue, so the tower does not merely get shorter, it visibly approaches something. LIMITED's whole tension is the ending, and today nothing on screen builds toward it.
It is one element. A repeating gradient and a height: no per-die nodes, no reflow, and the same 34×36px footprint the bag already occupies (22×26 in landscape).
Cost: it stops looking like dice. The current pile's face-down shells say "these are dice you have not seen yet", which is a real part of the mode's flavour, and a plate tower says "resource meter" — closer to a health bar than to a bag. At 1.5px per plate the last few are also very small; the base marking has to carry that, and on a low-DPI screen the plate lines may alias into a solid block.
