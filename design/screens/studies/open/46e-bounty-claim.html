What it says: you did this, and here is where. The bank is the only score in the game with no visible source — it is a number that grew for reasons that scrolled past. Marking the seats turns it into something you can audit by looking, and it puts the mode's identity on the board, which docs/MODES.md requires of every mode: a rule the player cannot see operating is a rule they will think is a bug.
Permanence for free. The mark is never removed, so the board says "never resets" without a word of copy.
It reads at a glance as pressure. A half-claimed enemy board is a picture of how the match has gone that no pair of numbers can give you.
Cost: a refilled seat has nowhere to keep the mark — a die covers its slot exactly — so the claim has to move onto the die's own corner, which is a mark of yours riding on their die and a second treatment to design. It also puts a permanent mark on someone ELSE's board — the one place in this game a player's own colour has never gone — and by the late match a busy board can carry six or eight of them, competing with dice, column chips and the row rails for exactly the same pixels. Under a refilled seat the ✦ survives only in the slot's margin, which is 6px, so it has to be legible at that size or become clutter with no meaning.
