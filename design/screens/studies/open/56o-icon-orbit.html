Why an orbit. The shipped icon is one die, and this study keeps it — the same five at the same 7° — and gives it the opponent as a satellite: a small two of their hue on a thin ring that passes behind my die and comes back in front. One mark, two owners, and the shipped silhouette still at its centre for anyone who already knows the icon. The ring is drawn; the dice are the app's own. Faces are fixed by the design — the profile avatar never moves them. The dark appearance keeps the charcoal ground; my hue glows under the centre and the opponent's under their moon.
