What it says: chance was re-woven. FATE is the one rune that reaches into the supply rather than onto the board, and this is the only idea in the set whose picture is about that rather than about logistics — the die is unmade and something else is made in its place.
It has no geometry. Nothing travels, so there is no lane to reserve, no rect to resolve at cast time, and no collision with the LIMITED bag on the left or the rune rail on the right. It is identical in portrait, landscape and the widget.
It is the engine that is already here. ui/fx burst() throws these particles on every multiplied placement and every strike; this holds them in place instead, so it inherits the reduced-motion skip and the embed-root handling for free.
Cost: it is PILFER's motes with a different hue (design PI4), and two runes sharing a signature is how a roster stops being legible — a player who has seen a die come apart into motes should not have to check the colour to know which spell just fired. It also says nothing whatsoever about the supply, so under LIMITED, where the cast costs the bag a die, the bag simply ticks for no visible reason.
