What it says: how much of the box is empty. Today the picture answers "roughly which quarter"; this answers "how much room is left", and empty space is a large low-frequency area the periphery resolves without fixating — unlike a detail inside a 24px tile. It adds no element, no motion and no hue, so reduce-motion, colour blind mode and all seven pickable player hues are irrelevant by construction.
It is the shipped bag, moved. Same shells, same bodies, same shadows, same integer offsets — only the group's top position changes, and transform is already transitioned in variants.css, so the motion costs nothing.
Cost — and this one was earned the hard way. The first version of this idea multiplied the fan's offsets by the ramp so the shells converged as the bag emptied, and an adversarial pass killed it: a tightening stack reads as MORE material in peripheral vision, so the two percepts fought and the wrong one won. Sinking removes that inversion, but the honest limit stands — 12px of travel is a foveal readout, not a peripheral one. It is a real improvement on the current quantisation and it will still not tell you anything from the corner of your eye. It also needs the window's top edge drawn at roughly .45 alpha or the crown has nothing to be measured against.
