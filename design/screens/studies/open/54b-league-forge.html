The direction: a group is a material, not a badge swap. Promotion spends one decisive forge beat: STONE fractures, heats and returns as twelve BONE plates around the same player die. Demotion deliberately refuses that triumphal violence — the thin SILVER coat cools and sheds, leaving the IVORY core and the player's identity untouched.
The deck is mandatory but finite. There is no close control on any frame. A swipe rail, numbered dots, Back/Next and the final Continue expose exactly one way through; the first frame always names the new group, then each promotion unlock gets one slide while demotion combines reversible and permanent consequences in one comparison. The result remains underneath and Continue returns to it.
What repeats and what does not: the material crossing plays on every promotion and every demotion. BONE's three mode explanations are first-unlock slides backed by the registry's own content. The SILVER boundary slide returns whenever equipped-rune access is lost or regained, while its permanent-unlocks section makes the non-loss explicit.
Motion contract: both 6.4-second studies complete by 52% and rest in the finished state for the remainder. Under prefers-reduced-motion, animations disappear and those same base styles render BONE fully assembled or IVORY fully settled — no alternate graphic, flash, or intermediate material.
