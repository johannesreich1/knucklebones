Why a split. The shipped mark is one die that follows the profile avatar, so 42 players own 42 launchers. This one is one die owned by two players: the same six, cut on a seam, my hue on the left pip column and the opponent's on the right. The face and its pips are fixed by the design — the profile never moves them; only the two hues from Settings are the player's. The dark appearance keeps the charcoal ground; only the hue lit under each half of the die changes.
