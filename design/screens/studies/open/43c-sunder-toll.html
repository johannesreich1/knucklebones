What it says: this has already been counted. It is the only idea here that answers the question a player actually asks before spending their one charge — is it worth it? — with a number instead of a mood. −16 beside their 47 is the whole cast in one glance.
Quieter, not louder. The doomed dice go grey and ruled-through while everything else keeps its colour, so the mark survives the gold by removing it: a pair that was multiplying a second ago has visibly stopped being part of the score. No other idea here can make that particular point.
The die in hand stays calm on purpose — a rim and a count, no throb. If the readout is the effect, a die competing for the eye is noise.
Cost, and it is a design decision, not a bug: this hands the player arithmetic they could have done themselves. It is a genuine strength change, not just a coat of paint, and it is the reason a rehearsal of it should be played before it ships. The −16 also has to live in the score cluster, which reserves, never collapses (§7) — it needs the BOUNTY star's lane or it will shove the total sideways every time it appears.
