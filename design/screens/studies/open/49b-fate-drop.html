What it says: you are committed before you know. FATE is the only rune in the roster that cannot be pressed again to take it back, and the reason is that it REVEALS — so an animation that shows the commitment landing first and the face arriving second is drawing the actual rule rather than decorating it.
It fits everywhere. One vertical axis, nothing beside the stage, so it is identical in portrait and landscape and cannot collide with the LIMITED bag on the left or the castable rune on the right — the constraint that costs FA1 its lane.
The blank die is already the game's word for "not yet known" — ui/bag.ts draws exactly that shell for the dice still in the bag — so the face-down beat needs no new vocabulary.
Cost: the stage briefly holds a die with no face, and a blank die on the stage is close to the "no die rolled yet" state the game shows between turns. It is also a beat longer than every other idea here — fall, drop, turn — and FATE can be cast twice a match, so that length is paid for twice on top of the turn it interrupts.
