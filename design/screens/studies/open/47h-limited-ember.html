What it says: the less there is, the hotter it gets. An emptying supply usually fades; this one brightens, which is the honest reading of a mode whose last four dice decide the match. The draw and the ending are the same light: the die rises out of the pool carrying it and cools into its own face as it lands.
The shells thin instead of the number. Solid while there are plenty, hollow when there are not — the die's shell is a gradient plus a border, so hollowing is one layer removed rather than a new treatment. The count keeps its colour, its size and its own gold tick.
The pool is the mode's, not a player's. LIMITED's registry hue (ui/modeicons) is a raw token, so it can never be mistaken for either seat's dice — and the SIZE of the pool, not its colour, is what carries the reading, which is what keeps it working in colour blind mode.
Cost: it puts a growing light 16px from the die in play, and the centre lane is the one place this game keeps quiet on purpose. At one die left the pool is the brightest thing there after the die itself — which is either the point or a distraction from the decision the player is making, and that is a question for a real phone rather than a card. Hollow shells also stop reading as dice at 24px, so the last few draws lose the "these are dice you have not seen" joke.
