What it says: this mode turns dice into money. The mode's own glyph is what lands on the face, so the animation names the rule that is operating — a player who has never read the sheet learns that the ✦ beside the score and the mark on the dying die are the same thing.
It costs nothing in the lane. Everything happens inside the seat the die already occupied: no travel, no second rect, no object crossing the centre stage. It is the same shape at three dice or six.
It uses the mark the registry already owns — {{mico:bounty:11}} comes from ui/modeicons, so a re-skin of the mode re-skins this and cannot drift.
Cost: it rewrites the shared death. .die.dying is one language across every mode, and a die that presses flat instead of tumbling away is BOUNTY editing something CLASSIC owns — defensible, but it is a per-mode fork of the most-seen animation in the game. It also says nothing about WHERE the point went: the tally still just changes.
