NONE
No rune — the pure game
The seat is empty by choice and ranked plays fine without it. Open any rune you have collected and equip it there; it takes this seat from SILVER upward.
What it says: the rune rides with your standing. The ring is the ladder made visible and its 90° mouth already names your group, so a seat cut into that mouth says "this comes with you into ranked" without a sentence under it. The two jobs also stay apart — the grid stays a collection, the ring holds the loadout — so shell.ts:96-98 survives with only its second clause rewritten. Card 52f (EQ6) is the other half of this one design: the same disc, at plate scale, on Home's identity row.
Why it survives: the SILVER rule lives here. The ring is a current-points object, so a demotion greys the same disc without touching what is in it, which is right for a pool gated on your peak but labelled by your points. Track grey with the bloom withheld, plus a SILVER pill, reads "yours, not open yet"; full registry hue with its bloom reads "in play". One object, one tint, no extra copy.
Measured, not hand-waved: at the 384px stage .ringwrap is 206px, the 74px avatar's lower edge lands at y=140 and .gname's box starts at y=190 — 50px of clear air. The 34px disc spans 148-182, so 8px above and 8px below, and it clears .avedit (x 123-149) by sitting at x 86-120. Nothing was moved to make room, and the group name never had space for a neighbour anyway: OBSIDIAN at 15px/.18em is ~94px of a chord measuring ~120px at the band. Nothing under the ring moved in this revision, so all of that still measures what it says.
The old cost is gone: this card used to admit a two-step gesture and a mode — the grid entering a choosing state, six slots meaning "pick me" instead of opening the library, and CLEAR wanting a control on a head row with no slot for one. None of that is drawn any more. A grid slot does what it does today, opening that rune's sheet (account-runes.ts:66-80), and EQUIP lives on that sheet — EQ1's mechanic, with UNEQUIP on the one already seated, so NONE stays reachable without a new control. The seat opens the same sheet in whichever of the three presentations above it is in. One tap each, no mode, nothing added to .accrunes-head.
Cost: the ring is already the busiest object in the app — track, fill, peak notch, avatar, pencil badge, group name — and this makes a sixth mark inside it. And the NONE sheet has nothing to open: SPELL_LIB.items is built by spellItems() from core's six runes, so there is no NONE entry and openEntry returns false on an id the roster does not hold (library.ts:191). That door needs a direct showSheet({cls:'libsheet', label, body}) with no tint — the third sheet above — or a NONE pseudo-item in the library spec. The parts are already lying there, since the offline picker's first slice uses spellCopy('none'), spellIcon('none') and spellHue('none'); they are the picker's roster, not the library's, and joining them is a decision rather than wiring. It is the one new runtime seam this idea needs.
The state easiest to get wrong: nothing equipped. Drawn as absence it reads as a bug in the ring; drawn as a fault it argues with the premise that "none" is a choice. It is drawn instead the way .pplate.anon is — dashed, --dim, the app's own shape for a place with nobody in it (identity.css:104) — and the same dashed disc heads its sheet, so the thing you tapped is the thing that answers. Three stills, not six: first rune, it seats itself and after a Ritual, untouched are this seat EQUIPPED, one frame under three captions, and not collected is a state the seat cannot reach at all. The auto-equip is still worth announcing — announcing it is card 52h (EQ8), not a second copy of this disc.
