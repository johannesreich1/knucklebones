What it says: it was struck. ANVIL is the only rune that changes a die already on the board, and a hammer is the one image that makes a die change its face without moving — which is the rule, exactly: nothing moves, nothing dies, the column keeps its height.
The mark answers "which one" before the tap. A raised hammer hanging over the chosen die is a prediction the player can check; the roster's promise is that ANVIL's target is never a surprise, and a ring around the whole column does not keep it. It also survives on a gold die, because it sits above the face rather than on it.
The column jumps. Two dice reacting to a blow landing between them is what turns three tiles into one object — and the score that changes is the column's, not the die's.
Cost: the falling wedge needs ~90px of clear air above the struck die, and the top row of your own half has the centre stage right above it. On the struck die in the TOP slot the hammer would fall through the die in play. It either shortens (and reads as a tap, not a blow) or comes in at an angle.
