What it says: six positions, one step, and the ring closes. The wrap is the hardest thing about this rune to explain in words — the sheet needs a clause for it — and a ring explains it without one: the 6 is beside the 1 because a circle has no end. A player who casts on a 6 once will not be surprised twice.
It never touches the die. The face swaps and everything else the player knows about a die in hand — size, colour, position, the drag target — is untouched, which matters for the one rune whose cast target is the die itself.
It is reusable. A bounded-step rune added later gets the same ring with a different notch count.
Cost: it is chrome around the die in play, and that box is already crowded — the LIMITED bag hangs 16px to its left, the castable rune 16px to its right, and in landscape both are squeezed into a 116px lane (--land-lane). A ring at 0.78× the cell would sit under both. It also asks the player to read a second object at the moment they are reading the face, and it says nothing at all about WHY the tick was worth casting.
