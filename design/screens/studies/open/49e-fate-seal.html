What it says: that is done. The reason FATE's cast cannot be reconsidered is subtle — it revealed the next die, so putting it back would be a free look paid for with nothing. The seal is the game already having an answer for "this cannot be touched": the protected column wears a closed line, and a closed line means the same thing here.
It is the only idea aimed at the moment that surprises people. A player who has just watched a die they did not choose land in their hand reaches for the rune again; today it simply refuses them, silently, which reads as a bug rather than as a rule.
It layers. The exchange underneath is deliberately the plainest in the set, so this can sit on top of FA2's drop or FA4's pass rather than competing with them.
Cost: a ring around the die in play is a mark the game already uses for something else — html.castself puts a dashed aim ring on exactly that box while a self spell is being aimed — so the seal has to be unmistakably a different line, or the player reads "you may cast here" one frame after they have. It also asks a lot of a small mark: "final" is a rule, and rules are usually taught by copy, not by a border.
