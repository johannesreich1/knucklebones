What it says: this die came out of that bag. LIMITED's rule is not "there is a counter" — it is that every die on the table was taken from one shared, finite supply, and the counter is only the bookkeeping. Making the draw a single movement states the rule on every single turn, twenty-four times a match, without a word.
It fixes the mode's dead frames. The pile only redraws itself on four draws out of twenty-four (ui/bag.ts quantises to four layers), so most turns the bag currently does nothing at all. Here the bag moves every time, because a die actually left it.
Face-down until it lands — the die turns over at the top of its arc, which keeps the bag's one promise: it tells you how MANY, never which.
Cost: it puts a beat in front of the roll. The stage die's own reveal (ui/game/motion animateStageRoll) already owns that moment and already has a rattle, a scramble and a settle, so this either replaces that animation under LIMITED — a per-mode fork of the most-played animation in the game — or is added in front of it and the turn gets slower. In landscape the bag sits in a 116px lane (--land-lane) with the rune bar, so the arc has to be re-measured, not re-used.
