Why a clash. The shipped mark is one die that follows the profile avatar, so 42 players own 42 launchers. This one is the rule that decides every match: my four meets the opponent's four at the corner, and the gold glint is the knockout. Faces and pips are fixed by the design — the profile never moves them, and neither seat is the player's to change. The dark appearance keeps the charcoal ground; each owner's hue washes only its own corner and lights only its own die's rim, and the impact wash under the glint is the only warm light.
