The hands: a pile shuffle, the casino's cure for sequence memory — the fan is squared into one deck, dealt out one card at a time, round-robin, into three little piles (left, centre, right, then left again), the left pile is dropped onto the centre, the right pile on top of that, and the rebuilt deck fans back out. Card 1 and card 4 leave on different laps and land in the same pile; no other table gesture states "the order changed" this plainly. Beats: FAN 380 → SQUARE 280 → PILE ×6 1380 (230ms cadence, 170ms flick, strictly one card in flight) → STACK 730 (two ~270ms hops) → REFAN 510 → DEAL 560 → FLIP 520 — shuffle portion 2900ms, whole beat 4360ms (today 1250 / 2900). The loop above plays each shuffle pass ~18% slower than proposed so each flick stays readable, and holds each settled fan for a seventh of the loop. Honest re-order: redeal()'s icon swaps land once per card, at the instant it leaves the combined stack on the REFAN — the same seam riffle() uses at its half-beat — so the fan is rebuilt card by card already wearing the interleave the piles just performed, and the draw reaches into a slot this pass visibly decided. The loop above therefore plays the shuffle twice — pass one deals the fan out through the pile permutation, pass two, a mirrored deal, brings every rune home — because a CSS study cannot relabel runes the way redeal() does; in the app a single pass plus redeal()'s icon swaps achieves the same visible re-order. Cost vs S1: the longest of the eight and the most serial — one card in flight at a time is the whole argument, but it is also a metronome, and by the tenth reveal it may read as waiting where S1's retimed riffle still reads as flourish; it is the most new choreography (six flicks, two hops) and needs ±96px of felt side-room, the most of the set (still inside the 58px --card floor). Seam: still WAAPI in ui/runedeal.ts — riffle()/cut() give way to pileDeal() and stackPiles() reading the same restOf(); the three pile anchors are one local const; restOf() unchanged; Sfx.tick per flick, Sfx.place per hop.
