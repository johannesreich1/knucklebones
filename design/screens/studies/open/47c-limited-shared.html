What it says: there is one bag and you are both in it. This is the mode's most-missed rule — the blurb says "one shared bag of 24" and players hear "24 each" — and it is also the reason LIMITED plays the way it does: taking the die you wanted denies it to them. A draw that visibly serves whoever is to move says so twice a round, forever, and needs no copy.
The count falls on their turn too, in front of you, which today happens silently while your attention is on their board.
It reuses one gesture — the same pull, mirrored — rather than inventing a second animation for the opponent. Faces rotate 180° on the far side already (ui/dom faceRotated), so the drawn die arrives correctly oriented for its owner.
Cost: it animates on the opponent's turn, which is the one time the game currently keeps the centre quiet so the player can read the enemy board. It also doubles the number of draws that carry motion — forty-eight per match — and in landscape the two halves are left and right, not up and down, so the direction has to be re-derived from the seat rather than hard-coded.
