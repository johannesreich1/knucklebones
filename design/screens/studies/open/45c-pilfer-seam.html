What it says: a border was crossed. This is the only idea that answers the steal and the refusal with the same object: the die passes THROUGH the centre line and it ripples, and the stretch of that line in front of the shielded column is drawn unbroken and gold. "You may not rob a shielded column" stops being a ring that failed to appear and becomes a wall that is visibly there.
It gives the centre line a job. The game draws nothing between the halves today — the two hairlines were removed by request, and only the glow palette separates them. A border that exists solely while an aggressive rune is armed is a use of that space that costs nothing for the rest of the game.
Cost, and it is a scope problem more than a design one: a membrane between the halves is a new object in the busiest 26px on the screen (the die in play, the rune rail, the LIMITED bag and the status line all live in that lane), and it belongs to the WHOLE table rather than to one rune — which means it will want to say something during SUNDER and WARD too, and this study cannot decide that. It also has nothing to say in landscape, where the halves sit side by side and the seam is vertical.
