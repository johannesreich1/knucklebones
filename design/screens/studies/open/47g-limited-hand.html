What it says: this die came out of that pile, and the pile is running out. LI1 and LI4 were each half of the mode — a draw with no stakes, or stakes with nothing happening on the twenty draws before them. One bag doing both is the first version of this that is alive on every turn AND builds toward its ending.
The count is untouched. The escalation is carried by the shells: they take the mode's own hue, the fan closes as the supply squares up, and the last few breathe. The number keeps its size, its colour and its 450ms gold tick exactly as ui/bag.ts ships them, and neither nameplate changes at all.
It tints the token the app already tints. --dc is the die's own colour, so a warming shell is the same move the game makes for a multiplied die — not a new treatment to learn.
Cost: the shells stop being neutral, and their neutrality was doing a job — a face-down die in nobody's colour is what makes the bag feel anonymous. Tinting them toward violet also has to be checked against colour blind mode, where the duel pair is pinned to cyan-and-gold and a violet that reads as "nearly out" must not read as a player's dice. The closing fan is 3px of movement at the size of a fingernail, so it may only register in the strip and not in play.
