What it says: this is what you are holding. It is the only idea in the set where the resting card answers that question on its own — the index is large enough to identify without turning anything — and the only one that keeps the rune's NAME in the rail once it is played.
Spent is legible from across the table. An ashen, scored face reads as used at a glance and from any angle; a dimmed card reads as a card.
Cost: it is the one card in the set that stands TALLER than the die in play — 44px wide and 60 tall against a 57px die — which is exactly the rule the other five keep. In landscape the whole centre lane is 116px (--land-lane) with the status line and the LIMITED bag in it. Its 7.5px label also has to be re-checked at every device size rather than assumed.
Face-to-face: in portrait duo the card turns 180° with the rest of the centre — styles/game/seating.css already rotates #dieStage, .status and .timer for whoever is playing, and the card belongs in that selector. No new mechanism, one more name in a rule that exists.
