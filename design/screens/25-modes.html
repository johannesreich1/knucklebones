GAME MODES
Every ranked match spins the wheel — these are the slices
CLASSIC50%
The pure duel. Columns multiply.
Matching dice stacked in a column multiply: two 4s = 16, three 4s = 36. Place a die and every matching die in the facing enemy column is destroyed. First full grid ends it — highest total wins.
ROW SWITCH10%
Scoring turns sideways — only rows count.
Only ROWS score here — columns count for nothing. Matching dice in the same row multiply, and the rail on the left tracks every row. Destruction still strikes down the facing column.
ROW MULTIPLY10%
Rows pay a bonus on top of columns.
Columns score as always — and matching dice lined up in a ROW pay their sum again on top. The rail on the left shows what each row is adding.
COLUMN SHIELD10%
A full column cannot be destroyed.
Fill a column and it locks: a shielded column cannot be destroyed, whatever lands opposite. The shield pops onto the column chip the moment it engages.
SINGLE STRIKE10%
Destruction takes ONE die — the closest to the centre.
Destruction is surgical: a hit removes only ONE matching die — the one closest to the centre. Stacks survive longer, so multipliers rule the board.
BOUNTY10%
Every die you destroy banks +1. Forever.
Every die you destroy banks a permanent +1 on your nameplate — the ✦ tally never resets, even when your own dice fall. Feed on destruction; the bank decides close matches.
