The ladder gets an actual rail. One continuous spine runs the whole board and changes colour exactly where a group begins — the boundary happens where it happens, labelled in the margin, not promoted to a header row. Ranks live ON the rail as knots (the top of each group stays bright, the rest sit dim), so a glance down the left edge reads the season's shape the way the profile ring reads one player's. Your knot wears the cyan outline even as a thumbnail. A tapped row grows a popover pinned to itself — the detail appears where the question was asked and scrolling on simply closes it. Costs: the rail takes a 34px column from every row, and the joint gradients need care so the rail never looks broken between groups.
