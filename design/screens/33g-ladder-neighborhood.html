Show the whole board
The board opens where the player actually looks: at themselves. Rank 1 is the summit, but the race you are IN is against the players a few dozen points either side — so the summit shrinks to three pinned medallions, the middle folds into one honest line, and the screen's centre is YOUR row, full size, with your group, your fill and your record. Every neighbour states the gap to you in points — magenta above, cyan below, the duel palette doing the talking — which is the number that decides tonight's session. The gap is a distance, never a payout: what a match is worth is not previewable in this game, by design. Tapping a neighbour opens the face-off card (study L5); the fold expands to the full strata list (study L1). Costs: it needs a signed-in player with games to centre on — signed-out or unranked, it degrades to the plain top-down board — and the server must return a window around your rank, not just the top 50 (player_standing() already knows the centre).
