The bottom of a screen = actions only

Every bottom stack holds actions, never navigation — and exactly one is primary: Play, Sign in, Keep it forever, Next duel, Cancel. “Back”, “Close” and “Done” as bottom buttons are retired everywhere. Matchmaking's only exit is Cancel, and cancelling truly leaves the queue.

In match — one control: the way out

The HUD's single button wears a doorway with an arrow leaving it — it was a sliders glyph from when it opened Settings, and kept promising a screen it no longer opened. It opens an ask-card, not a sheet: mid-match, sound and dice faces are not what you reach for, and the only thing worth offering is the exit. One question, a way back, and copy that knows the stake — Quit this game? offline, Forfeit this match? when a rating is live. The way back is the one wearing the gradient; the destructive answer is the quiet full-width one. The pass-the-phone screen keeps its own ✕.
